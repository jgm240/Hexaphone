Hexaphone

Android 9 for the Google Nexus 10. A rebranded, tinkerer-friendly LineageOS 16.0 build, with its own app store and updater.

Download latest View on GitHub

What is it?

LineageOS stopped at Android 7.1 for the Nexus 10 (manta). Hexaphone builds on the community's unofficial Android 9 port of this tablet and turns it into something with its own identity and tooling, so a 2012 device can keep being useful.

What you get

What ships in the current release.

Hexagon identity

A black boot animation with two hexagon rings spinning in opposite directions, a hexagon default wallpaper, hexagon app-icon shapes, a rebranded setup wizard, and dark mode by default.

Hexaphone Store

A small app store with a live catalog: it reads its list from a manifest online, so new apps show up without a new ROM build. Downloads are hash-verified before they install.

Hexaphone Updater

Browse every Hexaphone release, download one with checksum verification, and move forward or back to any version. It updates itself too.

Live wallpaper

The boot mark, slowly spinning, as a selectable live wallpaper.

In the works

Written and compiling, but not in a release yet.

One-tap flash

Updater becomes a built-in system app that can reboot straight into recovery and flash the zip it just downloaded.

System Tweaks

ZRAM and swap controls, a SELinux permissive quick-settings tile that resets on reboot, and manual backup and restore of your Hexaphone settings.

Bootscreen & wallpaper colors

Pick a boot animation color and speed, and recolor the live wallpaper to match.

Polish

A hexagon-grid app drawer, a "What's new" screen after each update, and, being finished now, a branded charging screen, a stats widget, a display font and a sound theme.

Download

The newest release is looked up live from GitHub. Every release has an MD5 checksum next to the zip; check it before you flash.

Install

Standard custom-ROM flashing. If you've flashed a tablet before, nothing here will surprise you.

  1. Back up anything you care about. Moving to Hexaphone from another ROM means wiping data.
  2. Unlock the bootloader and install a custom recovery such as TWRP for the Nexus 10.
  3. Download the zip above and verify its MD5 checksum.
  4. Copy the zip to the tablet, boot to recovery, wipe data, and flash the zip.
  5. Reboot. The first boot is slow because the system compiles itself on the device.

Already on Hexaphone? Open Hexaphone Updater, pick a release, and download it, then flash it from recovery the same way.

Known issues

This is an alpha, built on an unofficial port, and these gaps come with it. They haven't been fixed.

  • The camera is broken. HAL1 doesn't work and HAL2 isn't supported, so the camera app is removed.
  • Hardware composer is partly broken. Some reports of invisible icons, wallpaper glitches and overlay problems.
  • Don't enable disk encryption. It causes bootloops.
  • NFC is unreliable and can drain the battery, so NFC support is removed.
  • No Google apps. There is no Play Store; use Hexaphone Store, F-Droid or Aurora Store.
